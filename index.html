V-ZERO, however, completely destroys the molecular structure of harmful substances the instant they touch the catalyst surface, neutralizing them (ZERO).

Core Technology

가시광 · 무광 · 열촉매
3중 경로 동시 작동 Visible light, lightless, thermal —
three pathways working at once

기존 광촉매는 자외선(UV) 전용이나 V-ZERO는 가시광 · 무광 · 열 3중 경로로 실내 조명(500lux)에서도 작동합니다. Conventional photocatalysts work only with ultraviolet (UV) light, but V-ZERO works even under indoor lighting (500 lux) through three pathways: visible light, lightless and thermal.

기술 자세히 보기Technology details
Path 01 · Visible Light

가시광 촉매Visible-light catalysis

실내 조명(500 lux) 응답 — 실내 조명만으로 강력한 촉매 활성화.Responds to indoor lighting (500 lux) — powerful catalytic activation with indoor lighting alone.

Path 02 · Lightless

무광 촉매 (암촉매)Lightless (dark) catalysis

무광촉매(암촉매)가 빛 없이도 공기 중 O₂ · H₂O로 ROS를 생성하여 24시간 작동합니다.The lightless (dark) catalyst generates ROS from O₂ and H₂O in the air even without light, working 24 hours a day.

Path 03 · Thermal

열촉매Thermal catalysis

온도와 시간의 상승에 따라 CO 분해 능력이 향상됨 (열촉매 반응).CO decomposition capacity improves as temperature and time increase (thermal catalytic reaction).
